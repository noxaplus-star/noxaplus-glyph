# NoxaPlus Glyph Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NoxaPlus Glyph is a public, open-source project. NoxaPlus welcomes constructive participation from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, and abilities. Everyone should be able to contribute without harassment or discrimination, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, race, religion, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, appearance, or socioeconomic background.

This code applies equally to contributors, users, and maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, considerate, and patient, including across language and cultural differences.
- Critique ideas, code, and design decisions rather than people.
- Give useful, specific feedback and accept good-faith feedback without personal attacks.
- Ask for clarification before assuming intent, and acknowledge mistakes when they occur.
- Respect other people's privacy, time, accessibility needs, and right to disengage.
- Keep discussions relevant to NoxaPlus Glyph and use non-sensitive examples when sharing typography samples, screenshots, or project files.
- Follow repository contribution and security-reporting guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory insults, slurs, derogatory stereotyping, or exclusionary behavior.
- Sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, or inappropriate sexual material.
- Personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, hostile trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information, messages, or identifying data without permission.
- Spam, deceptive impersonation, malicious links, or attempts to exploit other participants.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or assists an investigation.

Reasoned disagreement and criticism are welcome. They must remain respectful and must not become harassment.

## Scope

This code applies to the NoxaPlus Glyph repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed communication spaces. It also applies when someone is representing NoxaPlus Glyph publicly, such as using an official project account or acting as an appointed project representative. It is not a claim of authority over unrelated private activity.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **[NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com](mailto:NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com)** with a subject such as **“NoxaPlus Glyph conduct report”**.

Include the relevant location, dates, links or evidence, a short account of what happened, and any immediate concerns. Share only the personal information needed to understand the report. Do not publish private details in a public issue. You may report behavior directed at you or behavior you witnessed.

Reports will be handled as discreetly as possible. Information may need to be shared with people responsible for investigating or addressing the incident; absolute confidentiality or a fixed response time cannot be promised. If there is an immediate danger, seek help from the appropriate local emergency service rather than relying on project maintainers.

Security vulnerabilities should follow the separate [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

NoxaPlus project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code within project-managed spaces. They may remove or edit harmful content and limit participation when necessary to protect the community.

Depending on severity, context, and repeated behavior, responses may include a private request to change behavior, a warning, removal of content, a temporary participation restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces. Serious threats or harassment may justify immediate restriction without prior warnings. Decisions should be proportionate, consider available evidence, and avoid retaliatory treatment of reporters.

Where practical, affected participants will receive an explanation of the decision and expectations for future participation. A participant may request reconsideration by emailing [NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com](mailto:NoxaPlus2025@gmail.com) with relevant new information. The project does not promise an independent appeals process. Maintainers are subject to the same conduct standards.
